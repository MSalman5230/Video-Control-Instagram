# Privacy Policy

Instagram Native Video Controls does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The extension runs only on `instagram.com` pages so it can find Instagram video elements and enable Chrome's native HTML5 video controls. It does not send page content, browsing activity, account information, video information, analytics, or identifiers to the developer or any third party.

The extension stores your last video volume and mute setting locally in the browser using Instagram's page storage. This setting stays on your device and is used only to apply your preferred volume to newly focused Instagram videos.

The extension does not load remote code, inject advertisements, or call external APIs.

For support or privacy questions, use the support contact listed on the Chrome Web Store item page.
